  1. Open this page in Safari (not inside another app’s browser).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen → Add. Noor opens full-screen from its own icon.

PC & Mac Your device

  1. In Chrome or Edge, click Install app above (or the install icon in the address bar).
  2. Noor opens in its own window and appears in your Start menu / Applications.

What you get

QuranVerified Tanzil Uthmani text, translations (English, Urdu, Hindi), bookmarks, notes and search.
Prayer times12 calculation methods, Hanafi/standard Asr, calculated on your device.
TasbeehCounter with targets, undo, history and focus mode.
Qibla & calendarBearing to the Kaaba and the Umm al-Qura Hijri calendar.
